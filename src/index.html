About the Club

History

Founded in 1892, Liverpool grew from a Second Division side under Bill Shankly into one of the most decorated clubs in the world.

Honours

Twenty English league titles and six European Cups, the most of any English club in Europe's premier competition.

The Kop

The famous Spion Kop stand is the heart of Anfield, where "You'll Never Walk Alone" rings out before every home game.

Club Legends

Click a legend to read more.

Bill Shankly

Shankly arrived in 1959 with Liverpool in the Second Division. He won promotion, three league titles, two FA Cups and the 1973 UEFA Cup, and built the foundations of the club's golden era.

Kenny Dalglish

"King Kenny" joined from Celtic in 1977 and scored the winner in the 1978 European Cup final. As player-manager he led Liverpool to the league and FA Cup double in 1985–86.

Steven Gerrard

A local lad from the academy who made over 700 appearances. His header started the famous comeback in Istanbul, where he lifted the Champions League as captain.

Mohamed Salah

Signed from Roma in 2017, the "Egyptian King" became one of the club's all-time top scorers and a multiple Premier League Golden Boot winner.
